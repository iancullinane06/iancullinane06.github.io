    Quick reference

    Symbols & equations

    Thermodynamic potentials

    U
    Internal energy; ΔU = Q + W (work done on the system).
    H
    Enthalpy, H = U + PV.
    G
    Gibbs free energy, G = U + PV − TS.
    F
    Helmholtz free energy, F = U − TS.

    State variables

    S
    Entropy; for a microcanonical system, S = kB ln Ω.
    T
    Absolute temperature, measured in kelvin (K).
    P
    Pressure, the force per unit area.
    V
    Volume occupied by the system.
    E
    Total energy; Ei is the energy of state i.
    N
    Number of particles; n is the amount of substance in moles.
    Ω
    Multiplicity: number of accessible microstates.

    Heat & processes

    Q
    Heat transferred to the system; positive when heat is supplied.
    W
    Work done on the system; positive for compression. For reversible pressure-volume work, δW = −P dV.
    CV
    Heat capacity at constant volume: (∂U/∂T)V.
    CP
    Heat capacity at constant pressure.
    γ
    Adiabatic index, CP/CV.
    Isothermal
    A process at constant temperature.
    Adiabatic
    A process with no heat transfer.
    Isobaric
    A process at constant pressure.
    Isochoric
    A process at constant volume.

    Statistical mechanics

    P(Ei)
    Probability of finding the system in energy state Ei.
    Z
    Partition function: Σi e−βEi.
    β
    Inverse thermal energy: 1/(kBT).
    ε
    Energy of the system in the reservoir example.
    Microstate
    A particular microscopic arrangement of a system.
    Macrostate
    A state described by macroscopic properties, potentially containing many microstates.
    Canonical
    An ensemble at fixed temperature whose system can exchange energy with a reservoir.

    Useful relations & constants

    Ideal gas
    PV = NkBT = nRT.
    First law
    ΔU = Q + W, with work done on the system positive.
    Entropy
    dS = δQrev/T for a reversible change.
    Carnot
    Reversible engine efficiency η = 1 − Tcold/Thot, with temperatures in kelvin.
    kB
    Boltzmann constant: 1.381 × 10−23 J/K.
    R
    Gas constant: NAkB ≈ 8.314 J/(mol K).
    NA
    Avogadro constant: approximately 6.022 × 1023 mol−1.
